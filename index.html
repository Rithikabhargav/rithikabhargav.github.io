Background

Building AI Beyond the Model

Artificial Intelligence is more than algorithms: it's the ability to transform data into intelligent systems that solve real-world challenges. My passion lies in building AI solutions that combine strong engineering principles with practical business impact.

My journey began in data engineering, where I designed secure databases, optimized SQL performance, and worked with large-scale enterprise data systems. That experience taught me an invaluable lesson: every successful AI solution starts with reliable, well-governed data.

Driven by a desire to build intelligent applications, I transitioned into Machine Learning Engineering at Cyient, where I developed deep learning models for Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS). Working with camera, LiDAR, and RADAR data, I built computer vision models using TensorFlow and PyTorch while optimizing them for real-time performance. This experience showed me how AI extends far beyond model training: it requires scalable pipelines, efficient deployment, and continuous optimization.

Today, I'm pursuing a Postgraduate Certificate in Applied AI Solutions Development at George Brown Polytechnic, where I'm expanding my expertise across the complete AI ecosystem. My projects span Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics, ETL pipeline development, Data Governance, SQL Server Integration Services (SSIS), Azure Data Engineering, MongoDB, Tableau, predictive analytics, and end-to-end AI solution development. Every project strengthens my ability to design intelligent systems that are scalable, explainable, and production-ready.

What drives me is the opportunity to bridge the gap between data, engineering, and artificial intelligence. I enjoy transforming complex datasets into meaningful insights, developing intelligent applications that solve real problems, and continuously exploring emerging technologies that shape the future of AI.

As I continue to grow as an AI Engineer, my goal is simple: to build innovative, responsible, and impactful AI solutions that create lasting value for people, businesses, and society.
